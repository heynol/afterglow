# Shotlist: phone-camera footage

Screen recordings can't show what makes Afterglow fun: the light in a dark room, the soundbar thumping, the remote
in hand. These shots need a phone camera. They're for the release page, the portfolio and social posts.

**Music rights:** use a track you're allowed to publish (your own, CC-licensed, or e.g. from the YouTube Audio
Library). The screen captures in `docs/media` and `dist/tv` use an NCS track (credit in the README) or show only
Afterglow's own picture.

**Setup:** room dark or dimmed, phone on a tripod or braced, 4K/30 or 1080p/60, exposure locked on the TV (tap and
hold), auto-HDR off. A shutter of 1/60 s or slower avoids TV flicker bands.

| # | Shot | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Establishing:** wide shot of the TV over the soundbar, music video playing, the corner preview fades in top right | 6–8 s | Static, slightly from the side; the preview's arrival is the moment |
| 2 | **The press:** over-the-shoulder, thumb presses OK on the remote, preview expands to full screen | 4–5 s | Remote in focus first, then pull focus to the TV if the phone allows |
| 3 | **Full screen, room glow:** wide, the room lit by Outrun or Lasers, beats visible on the walls | 8–10 s | Best with *Lasers*, *Liquid Dream*, *Outrun* |
| 4 | **Switching:** ◀/▶ through 3–4 visuals, cross-fades | 8 s | Tight on the screen, no room |
| 5 | **Carousel:** ▼ opens the carousel, browse, OK | 6 s | Tight on the screen |
| 6 | **Detail:** macro of the screen pixels of a scope visual (Phosphor, Lissajous) | 3–4 s | Shallow depth of field, optional |
| 7 | **Hand-back:** ▼ in the preview gives YouTube the remote back, preview slides away | 4 s | Shows that it stays out of the way |

**Edit:** 30–45 s, cut on the beat, the track's own audio. Export 1080p MP4 (H.264) for the release and a 6–8 s silent
loop as WebP for the portfolio.
